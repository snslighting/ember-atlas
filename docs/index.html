01 / THE MISSION

The view changes.
The planet doesn't.

MODIS and VIIRS see Earth differently. We bring their observations into a common daily grid, so you can explore the signals together.

From a single thermal anomaly to a regional pattern, Ember Atlas makes the connection visible — while keeping the source, uncertainty, and method in view.

01 / MODIS

A longer view.

Terra and Aqua's MODIS instrument detects thermal anomalies at approximately 1 km resolution. Its observations are part of a long Earth observation record.

A finer signal.

NOAA-20's VIIRS instrument captures smaller-scale thermal anomalies at a nominal 375 m resolution. Greater detail brings a different detection count.
