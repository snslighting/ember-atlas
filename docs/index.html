A TRANSPARENT BASELINE

From separate signals to a shared calendar.

01 / Normalize

Preserve location, UTC date and time, confidence, sensor, and fire radiative power.

02 / Filter

Apply identical region, date, and normalized confidence thresholds to both sensors.

03 / Aggregate

Group detections into approximate 1 km cells per UTC day; retain sensor membership and observation counts.

04 / Surface patterns

Flag days above the selected window’s mean + 1.5 standard deviations.

Exploratory baseline, not a validated scientific model. The latitude-adjusted grid is approximate and has boundary artifacts. Confidence scales are mapped heuristically; cloud and overpass coverage are not corrected. FRP uses the maximum observation per cell, not a summed energy estimate. A zero means no selected detections, not proof of no fire. Critical-day flags describe this window, not historical anomalies or safety alerts.
